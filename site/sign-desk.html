Sign Desk

Sign a PDF or a photo on any device, anywhere: your signature, initials, the date and ticks.

Or drag it onto this page, or copy it and press Ctrl+V. Word files: save them as PDF first.

Your signatures and initials

To add one, open a document and press Signature or Initials, then + New. Give each a name, such as HDFC or SBI; while signing, tap a name to choose which one goes on the page.

Signed copies in Google Drive

    Deleting, by hand or by the clear-after setting, moves a copy to Google Drive's Trash, where Google keeps it 30 days. The setting is shared by your devices.
